# Getting started

## Sign in

Open your Engenty workspace and sign in with your email and password. You'll land
on the home view with the sidebar on the left.

## Open the copilot

The copilot is available from anywhere in the app. Open it and ask a question or
describe a task in plain language — it can navigate the app, run module actions,
and search your prior conversations.

## Next steps

- Explore the modules enabled in your workspace from the sidebar.
- Visit **Admin → Agents** to see the agents available to the copilot.

This guide will grow with task-by-task walkthroughs.
