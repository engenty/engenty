# German chart of accounts (SKR 03)

**Standard:** DATEV SKR 03 (process chart SKR 04 is the other common DATEV
plan). This pack uses **SKR 03** because class **4 is operating expenses** —
the layout small firms and this product's expense list expect.

SKR 04 puts expenses in class **6** and revenue in class **4**. Do not mix them.

align with their Steuerberater's company plan.
